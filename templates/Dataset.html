🔬 Dataset Composition

Our dataset includes high-quality images of various agricultural pests captured in real farming conditions taken from IP102 Dataset (link). Each image is carefully labeled and verified by agricultural experts to ensure maximum accuracy.

  • High-resolution pest images (224x224 standardized)
  • Multiple angles and lighting conditions
  • Expert-verified classifications
  • Balanced distribution across species
  • Real field conditions captured
  • Seasonal variation included

🎯 Data Quality & Validation

To ensure the highest quality dataset, we implement rigorous validation processes including expert review, cross-validation, and continuous dataset refinement based on real-world performance feedback.

  • 80/20 train-validation split
  • Stratified sampling for balanced classes
  • Data augmentation for robustness
  • Expert agricultural validation
  • Continuous dataset updates
  • Cross-regional pest variants
